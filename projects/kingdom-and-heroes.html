Kingdom and Heroes

Turn-Based Board Game • Strategic Systems

Project Overview

Kingdom and Heroes is a turn-based board game that challenges players to balance castle fortification with tactical offensive maneuvers. The design focuses on high-level decision making through economic management and hero specialization.

Project Highlights

  • Core Systems: Integrated resource management with tactical castle combat logic.
  • Job & Skill System: Designed a multi-class system with unique skills for varied combat strategies.
  • Economic Design: Balanced a dual-resource loop for both castle building and recruitment.
  • Tactical Logic: Implemented board-game style positioning and turn-order systems.
