Rocket Ride

Physics-Based Puzzle • Unity • C#

Project Overview

Rocket Ride is a physics-centric puzzle game where players pilot a high-momentum rocket through narrow corridors and gravity-defying obstacles to reach a landing zone. The project explores the balance between thrust-based controls and environmental puzzle solving.

Project Highlights

  • Thrust Mechanics: Developed a physics-based propulsion system using Unity's Rigidbody2D and AddForce logic.
  • Collision Handling: Implemented velocity-based impact detection to determine safe landings versus explosive crashes.
  • Environmental Puzzles: Designed levels featuring gravity fields, moving obstacles, and fuel-management constraints.
  • VFX & Feedback: Integrated particle systems for rocket exhaust and screen-shake effects to enhance the sense of weight and power.
  • Level Progression: Balanced a difficulty curve that introduces tighter navigation and timing requirements as the player advances.
